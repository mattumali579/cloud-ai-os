# BrightReach Sales Message V1

## Headline
**Wake up to qualified homeowners already booked on your calendar for roof inspections.**

## Subhead
BrightReach builds and runs the ads, lead capture, qualification, follow-up, and booking system so your team spends less time chasing leads and more time closing roofs.

## What we are NOT selling
Do not lead with AI, automation, Higgsfield, n8n, agents, or software.

## What we ARE selling
- more qualified roof-inspection opportunities
- a predictable pipeline
- faster response to new leads
- less owner/admin follow-up work
- visibility into what ads and offers are producing appointments

## Founding offer
**Booked Roof Estimate Engine**
- $500 launch
- client funds ad spend directly
- $100 per qualified booked roof inspection
- month-to-month
- if the system is not live and booking-ready within 7 days after required access/assets are supplied, refund the launch fee

Qualified booked inspection means:
1. homeowner/property decision-maker
2. property is inside the contractor's service area
3. roof-related need exists
4. valid phone/email
5. appointment is placed on the agreed calendar
6. duplicate/spam/test leads do not count
