For advertising or visual creative tasks, treat the creative standard as mandatory project context. The GOOD reference is the default quality bar; the BAD reference is a reject pattern.

For outreach work, an explicit assignment from the owner counts as send approval after sender readiness is verified. Do not ask for duplicate approval unless a platform-required security step or real blocker exists.
