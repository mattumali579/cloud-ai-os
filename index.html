Gesture hologram

Hold both hands in the camera to stretch a colored portal between them. Drop a hand to hide it. Press Next look to flip through 15 color looks.

On a phone: same Wi-Fi as this computer. Open the Network address this page prints, allow the camera, then Start camera. This computer has to stay on.

Palm down + claw starts ROOM. Palm up + claw starts Rasengan. In ROOM, hold thumb + index + middle toward the camera until it says ARMED, then flip so the back of your hand faces the camera to swap. A tight closed fist with the thumb across the fingers shuts ROOM or Rasengan off. Then open the hand before starting another.

Range is camera-to-printed-ArUco only. Tape the marker to an object to range that object. Unmarked objects are not measured. If the marker is missing, the reading is distance unavailable.
